Pictures around you

Hung pictures float around you behind the people in the video, like posters on the walls of a room, and your backdrop fills the rest. A new picture goes where you're looking. In VR, point at a picture and hold the trigger to move it.

      Backdrops

      Photo wall arranges all your pictures to fill the see-through area, however many you add. Tap a single picture to use it alone instead: wide 2:1 panoramas wrap all the way around, other photos become one big wall. Pictures are kept in this browser, so they're here next time.

      From your laptop (Quest Link)

      1. Install the Meta Quest Link app on the laptop. In it, go to Settings → General and set Meta Quest Link as the active OpenXR runtime.
      2. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on the laptop and add videos and pictures from the laptop's drive.
      3. Connect the Quest with a USB-C cable or Air Link, and start Quest Link in the headset.
      4. Click Enter VR on the laptop and put the headset on. If the sound plays from the laptop, set Windows sound output to the Oculus headphones.

      On the Quest alone

      1. Connect the headset to your PC with a USB-C cable, choose Allow in the headset, and copy videos and pictures into Download.
      2. Open this page in the Quest Browser.
      3. Tap Add videos and Add pictures and pick files from the headset.
      4. Press Enter VR. The menu opens in front of you.
